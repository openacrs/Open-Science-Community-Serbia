On Wednesday September 16, 2026 workshop organized by Wikimedia Serbia titled "Copyright, Open Source Licenses, and Digitization of Research Materials on the Wikimedia Repository" will be held at the University of Belgrade - School of Electrical Engineering. Open Science Community Serbia (OSCS) is inviting PhD students, researchers and all interested parties to join the workshop, that will start at 11:30 AM on September 16, 2026 in room 314 (III floor of the building of Technical faculties).

Workshop Summary

The modern research environment requires not only the creation of high-quality scientific results, but also their responsible sharing within the framework of Open Science principles. This interactive workshop is tailored for PhD students, aiming to clarify the legal and technical aspects of sharing research materials on online platforms.
In the first part of the workshop, participants will be introduced to the legal frameworks governing the use and distribution of scientific content through Creative Commons licenses, the public domain and the "own work" rule. A special focus will be placed on analyzing the copyright status of diagrams, charts and field or laboratory photographs prior to publication.
The second part of the workshop is dedicated to practical hands-on work on Wikimedia Commons. Participants will master the technical steps of uploading, categorizing and digitalizing multimedia research material, adding Structured Data on Commons (SDC), as well as navigating the OTRS/VRT permission procedures for donating third-party materials for scientific purposes.

Speaker Biography

Nebojša Ratković is the Program Manager for Culture and Science at Wikimedia Serbia, leading projects that bridge the academic community, scientific institutions and cultural heritage organizations with open knowledge platforms. He holds degrees in political science and psychology, alongside master’s degrees in international humanitarian action and international economics, having completed his education in Belgrade, Bilbao and Dublin.
His professional focus encompasses Wiki project management, open knowledge methodologies and the application of Creative Commons licensing in the educational and research sectors. Serving as a consultant in international networks and a workshop facilitator, he specializes in interactive training on human resource management, soft skills, digitalization and the integration of modern digital tools into research and academic workflows.
