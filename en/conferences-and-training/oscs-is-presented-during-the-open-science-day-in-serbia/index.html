OSCS is presented during the Open Science Day in Serbia

From the presentation of OSCS, held during the Open Science Day V in Serbia, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14185169

Open Science Community Serbia (OSCS) is presented during the conference Open Science Day V in Serbia, that was held at the Rectorate of the University of Belgrade on November 5, 2024. During two sessions titled "Projects, networks, and initiatives", where librarians and researchers in Republic of Serbia presented their activities during the past two years, OSCS was also presented.

Open Science Community Serbia was established in 2021, while it shares Guiding Principles and Code of Ethics with other communities around the world that are gathered under the umbrella organization - International Network of Open Science & Scholarship Communities (INOSC).

For membership in OSCS, that is currently organized in form of a mailing list, it is required to fill out the Entrance Form available at: https://oscs.open.ac.rs/index.php/pristupnica?lang=sr.
