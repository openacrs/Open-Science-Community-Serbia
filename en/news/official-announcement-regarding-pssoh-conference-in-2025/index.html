Official Announcement Regarding PSSOH Conference in 2025!

The Editorial Board of the PSSOH Conference on the Application of Free Software and Open Hardware has regretfully decided that the event will not take place in 2025. We are, however, delighted to share that the next PSSOH Conference is scheduled to return in 2026. For the latest updates and details, please visit the News section on the official website of the PSSOH Conference.
