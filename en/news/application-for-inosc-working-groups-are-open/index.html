Application for INOSC working groups are open!

Photo by John Schnobrich on Unsplash

International Network of Open Science and Scholarship Communities (INOSC), as an umbrella organization of Open Science Communities (OSCs) including the Open Science Community of Serbia (OSCS), has initiated the practice of organizing working groups with the aim to bring together all interested members (as well as those who joined the OSCS mailing list) from all OSCs to promote selected topics and improve existing INOSC practices.

Anyone interested can view the list of working groups HERE. To register, enter the name and contact information (e-mail address) in the appropriate place provided for each working group.

A short presentation of the INOSC working group "Connecting Open Science Communities (OSCs) and Research Data Alliance (RDA)" is available at Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8075402.
