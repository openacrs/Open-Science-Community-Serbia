OSCS Wayback-style reconstruction

Based on user-provided screenshots of the archived oscs.open.ac.rs homepage and mobile menu.

Implemented:
- Open Sans typography
- white sticky header with subtle shadow
- larger OSCS logo
- compact uppercase desktop navigation
- generous white content spacing
- light-grey right-aligned footer based on archived layout
- centralised header/menu and footer in assets/js/menu.js
- dark, higher-contrast mobile slide-out navigation
- retained GitHub Pages-safe URL generation

Visual design:
